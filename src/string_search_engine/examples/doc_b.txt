Inventory at Vince's Vinyl is counted by hand every Monday morning before the doors open at ten. Every new arrival is graded for sleeve and disc condition before it gets a price sticker. This month the hip hop crates grew by sixty records after a local collector sold his entire basement stash. Country and folk titles now share one long shelf, which frees up space for a second listening station. Damaged sleeves are set aside in a clearance crate near the register and marked down by half. The part-time clerk suggested tracking weekly sales in a simple spreadsheet, so that slow sellers can be spotted early and returned to the distributor before the end of each quarter.
