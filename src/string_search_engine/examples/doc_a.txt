Vince's Vinyl started the week with about 1,200 records on the floor and another 300 boxed in the back room. Every new arrival is graded for sleeve and disc condition before it gets a price sticker. The jazz bins ran low after the Saturday sale, so Vince moved forty used reissues from storage to the front rack. Soul and funk sold steadily all week, while the classical section barely moved at all. Damaged sleeves are set aside in a clearance crate near the register and marked down by half. Vince wants a full count of the rock section before the holiday rush begins, because last winter the shop ran out of its most popular titles by early December.
